Thanks for testing TallyBee

TallyBee is a Google Sheet for US freelancers. It sorts your business expenses into Schedule C lines and tracks your quarterly tax payments. Your honest opinion decides what gets fixed before it goes on sale.

Your data stays with you

Your copy lives in your own Google Drive. TallyBee has no server, and the person who sent you this can't see your sheet. Never send anyone your bank files: only your answers on the form.

Set it up (5 minutes)

  1. Open your copy link and click Make a copy. The sheet and TallyBee are copied into your Google Drive.

  2. In your copy, wait about ten seconds for the 🐝 TallyBee menu next to Help, then click 🐝 TallyBee › 🏠 Home.

  3. Google asks for permission. Pick your account. Because this test copy hasn't been through Google's review yet, you'll likely see a warning. Click Advanced, then Go to TallyBee (unsafe). If Google shows checkboxes, tick Select all, then Continue.

Try these six things

Each one has something to look out for. Write down anything that surprises you, good or bad.

  1. See it with sample data

    On Home, click Try sample data. Look at the Transactions tab and the Tax Summary tab. When you're done, click Remove sample data on Home.

    does it make sense without anyone explaining it?

  2. Import a real bank file

    Download one to three months of transactions as a CSV from your bank, card, PayPal or Venmo website (usually under Activity or Statements, then Download). On Home, click Import a bank file, choose it, and type the account's name, like Chase Checking. No bank file to hand? Use the sample bank files.

    is money in and money out the right way round? Are the dates right? Is anything missing or doubled?

  3. Sort what's left

    Rows TallyBee couldn't place show in amber on Home. Click Show them, pick a category in the Category column, and answer Remember this rule? when it asks.

    did most of your rows already have the right category? Which ones were wrong?

  4. Add a bill

    On Home, click Add a bill. Upload a photo or PDF of a receipt (JPG, PNG or PDF), or type one in by hand.

    did it read the date, the store and the total correctly?

  5. Look at your taxes

    On the Setup tab, enter last year's total federal tax (from your last Form 1040) if you know it. If part of your home is only for work, try Home office square feet too. Then open the Tax Summary (your Schedule C, line by line) and Estimated Tax (your next quarterly payment).

    would you trust these numbers enough to show your tax preparer?

  6. Try to break it

    Import the same file twice. Type over a number in the Deductible column. Put a grocery bill under Office Supplies. If you use PayPal or Venmo, import that file and your bank's.

    does it catch you, and does it explain why? Is any money counted twice?

Already known, no need to report

  • It doesn't work in the Google Sheets phone app. Google doesn't run add-on windows there.
  • It doesn't connect to your bank automatically. That's on purpose: you never give anyone your bank login.
  • It covers federal tax for one Schedule C business. No state tax, rentals or farming.
  • It uses the 2026 IRS figures. The 2027 update comes once the IRS publishes them.

Not tax advice

TallyBee is a record-keeping tool built on published IRS rules, with sources. It doesn't prepare or file your return. Please don't file anything based on this test.
