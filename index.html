Questions, answered.

Is TallyBee tax advice?

No. It's a record-keeping tool built on published IRS rules, with the source shown for each one. Check your numbers with a tax professional before you file.

Does it file my return?

No. It gives you a Schedule C summary, line by line, to hand to your preparer or type into your tax software. You sign your return.

Where is my data?

In your own Google Sheet, in your Google account. TallyBee has no server and keeps no copy.

Does it work on my phone?

Not yet. The Google Sheets phone app can't open add-on windows, so use a computer for now.

Which taxes does it cover?

US federal tax for one Schedule C business. Not state tax, rentals, farming or clergy.

What does it cost?

One payment, no subscription. The price is set at launch.

TallyBee is a record-keeping tool, not tax advice. You sign your return. Google Sheets is a trademark of Google LLC.
